Who we are

A nationwide initiative, a local community

Established as part of a nationwide AICTE government initiative, our R&D Cell bridges the gap between academic learning and industry-relevant innovation. We provide the infrastructure, mentorship, and platform for students across every department to explore, experiment, and excel — whether that means building a prototype, publishing a paper, or simply learning to ask better questions.

Our Journey

How we got here

2021

R&D Cell founded

Launched under the AICTE initiative with a handful of student volunteers and two faculty coordinators.

2022

First funded project

Our smart water monitoring team secured our first external grant from the Department of Science & Technology.

2023

Webinar series launched

Began hosting regular webinars connecting students with researchers and industry professionals.

2025

National recognition

Reached the Smart India Hackathon finals and won a Best Research Paper award at an IEEE conference.
